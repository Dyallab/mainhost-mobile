# Changelog

Published Android builds. Each version links to its release.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-14

- New profile tab with your account, plan and active sessions.
- Revoke sessions from your phone.
- Android-only build with simpler versioning.
- Bug fixes and stability improvements.

[Release notes](https://github.com/Dyallab/mainhost-mobile/releases/tag/v0.4.0)

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-08

- Sign in with your MainHost account.
- Manage your game server: live status, resources, logs, backups and start/stop.
- First public beta APK.

[Release notes](https://github.com/Dyallab/mainhost-mobile/releases/tag/v0.1.0)

`0.2.x` and `0.3.x` were internal milestones and never produced a public APK.
